WhatsApp wasn't built for teams.

When customer messages land on one shared phone, things get missed. Nobody knows who replied to what. Follow-ups fall through the cracks. And sending a real campaign means copying and pasting to hundreds of people, one at a time.

BlueConnect fixes that — without changing the WhatsApp your customers already know.

Features

Everything your team needs in one place

Shared inbox

Every teammate sees the same conversations, in real time. Claim a chat, reply, and never lose track of who's talking to whom.

Campaigns

Send real WhatsApp messages to your whole contact list — approved templates, delivery tracking, and safe sending limits built in.

Automation

Set up keyword replies, welcome messages, and follow-ups that fire automatically — so nothing waits on a human who's offline.

Clear billing

See exactly what you're spending, per message, with a simple prepaid wallet — no surprise invoices.

Built for your systems

Already have a WhatsApp Business number, or a business system like an ERP or POS? BlueConnect connects to what you already run.

Real-time, always

New messages show up instantly for your whole team — no refreshing, no polling, no missed replies.
